## Two levels of claim

1. **Source statement:** "The image places X beside Y," "the author defines red as Reality," etc. These are claims *about the artifact*.
2. **Interpretive statement:** "This could model tension between personal and collective narratives," etc. These are creative analyses, not artifact facts or independently established scholarship.

When drawing an inference, mark it as an inference. Historic or theological explanations should be supported by references that explain each tradition on its own terms, and describe disagreement rather than smooth it over.

## How to compare symbolic traditions

- Define each term in its originating context first.
- State the narrow motif being compared: ascent, threshold, cyclical return, underworld, world axis, etc.
- Describe a *family resemblance* only where warranted; don't imply a shared origin or equivalence without evidence.
- Identify differences that matter. E.g., the Tibetan Buddhist bardo is not interchangeable with the ancient ouroboros symbol, and Norse Yggdrasil is not the same object as the biblical Jacob's Ladder.
- For a creative synthesis, explicitly label the result as Creagent's synthesis rather than the doctrine of any tradition.

## Example traces

### Viewer to oneness

Diagram-supported structure: `viewer → yggdrasil → eden → one`. This is a **designed symbolic route**, not a claim that consciousness can traverse a physical route to divinity.

### Moon to Sun

`moon → viewer → sun` uses *agent-authored navigation edges* to illustrate the move between inward and outward emphasis; the image draws them as left and right poles. It does not imply personal subjectivity becomes collective consciousness by any scientifically established process.

### Bardo and Astral

`bardo ↔ astral` is a source-defined boundary relationship. It records what the illustration states, not a verified account of dying.

## Unresolved / do not assume

- Exact scale, proportional geometry, or mathematical identity between circles.
- Whether every named minor realm should be represented as a separate layer.
- A single historical lineage for all cultural references.
- The physical meaning of black holes, wormholes, simulation, quantum, or white holes when used in this artwork.
- Ownership and licensing of the drawing. Do not republish it in the repo without permission.
